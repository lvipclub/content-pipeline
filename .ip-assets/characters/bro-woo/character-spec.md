# Bro Woo — Solo Character Spec (X post photo overlays, comics)

Solo Bro Woo for X post images and comic cutouts. Used when the deliverable is
ONE X-post image carrying a real photo: overlay Bro Woo ONLY (no Inu Faa) as a
~10% corner sticker. See `hero-image-prompt-construction` → Photo-Overlay section.

## Official Visual Assets
- Default identity reference: `assets/bro-woo-clean-reference.png` (RGBA cutout, transparent background)

## Fixed Identity Traits
- Young man, short black spiky hair — SUBTLE spiky, NOT cartoon lightning-bolt, NOT anime, NOT exaggerated
- Thin rectangular black glasses — ALWAYS on
- Grey V-neck sweater over white collared shirt, dark grey trousers, black shoes
- Friendly smile, lecturing/pointing pose (mirrored so he points INTO the scene when overlaid)
- Flat vector style, black line art, orange accent only

## Allowed Variation
- Pose, direction, slight expression — matching the overlay use case

## Forbidden Drift
- Never: anime/lightning hair, missing glasses, hoodie/t-shirt instead of grey V-neck, extra colours, second character (no Inu Faa in solo overlays)

## Reusable Identity Prompt (compact)
> Bro Woo: young man, subtle short black spiky hair, thin rectangular glasses, grey V-neck sweater over white collared shirt, dark grey trousers, black shoes, friendly smile, flat vector style, black line art, orange accent only.
