## Fixed Identity Traits (Bro Woo)
- Young man, short black spiky hair — SUBTLE spiky, NOT cartoon lightning-bolt, NOT anime, NOT exaggerated
- Thin rectangular black glasses — ALWAYS on
- Grey V-neck sweater over white collared shirt, dark grey trousers, black shoes
- Friendly smile; points at whiteboard with a small pointer when presenting

## Fixed Identity Traits (Inu Faa)
- EXACTLY ONE slim elegant Shiba Inu — tan cream fur, white chest/muzzle/paws, large pointed ears, curled fluffy tail
- Yellow collar with small orange lightning-bolt charm — ALWAYS visible
- Happy attentive expression, sitting upright beside Bro Woo
- NOT round, NOT chubby, NOT a second dog, no other animals

## Fixed Environment Style
- Pure white background, flat vector illustration, black line art, orange ONLY as accent colour
- Whiteboard (when present): simplified diagram, 2-5 elements, black lines + orange accents, short labels only
- Footer (when present): small "xinca.com" text bottom-right
- NO photorealism, NO gradients, NO shadows, NO textures, NO extra characters/props/text

## Allowed Variation
- Action, pose, direction of gaze, slight expression; whiteboard diagram content per article topic

## Forbidden Drift
- Never: second dog, missing glasses, exaggerated/lightning hair, chubby Inu Faa, missing collar+charm, extra colours beyond orange accent, dense text, full URLs as footer, "Network"/"Ethernet" words (FLUX mangles)
