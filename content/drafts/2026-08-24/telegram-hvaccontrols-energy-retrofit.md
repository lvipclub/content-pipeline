**The Retrofit Ladder: Why Fans and Pumps Come First**

HVAC is 40–50% of a commercial building's energy use — the biggest savings rarely sit in the chiller. Start with fans and pumps, three rungs:

1. **VSDs + resets.** Fan/pump power follows the cube of speed: a 20% speed cut saves nearly 50% energy. Pair drives with supply-air, static-pressure and CHW resets to run at real load, not design peak.

2. **Occupancy scheduling.** Fixed time clocks waste energy on empty floors. Zone-level occupancy or CO2 sensing with wider unoccupied setpoints cuts fan and chiller load.

3. **Sequence it.** Rungs 1–2 pay back in 2–4 years, cut base load, and right-size — or extend the life of — the chiller.

**What this means for your next project:** specify a VSD + reset package before a full chiller replacement. Verified savings fund the next rung.
