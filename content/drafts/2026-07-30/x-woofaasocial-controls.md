The gap between IAQ sensor specifications and real-world BMS integration is where most healthy building projects fail.

A CO₂ sensor that sends data every 15 minutes over a proprietary radio protocol is not the same as one that streams to BACnet MS/TP every 60 seconds for demand-controlled ventilation. The NDIR sensor itself costs the same — the integration cost difference is 3-4x.

Three specifications that separate a WELL-certifiable IAQ installation from a compliance checkbox:

1. BACnet MS/TP or Modbus RTU for every breathing-zone sensor — no proprietary bridges
2. Sub-60-second polling intervals for CO₂ — DCV responds faster, saves 25-35% outdoor air during partial occupancy
3. On-sensor calibration logging — annual drift compensation is documentation the commissioning agent will request

The healthy building specifiers who eliminated proprietary sensor ecosystems in 2024-2025 are now 6-9 months ahead on WELL v2 recertification cycles. The integration decision matters more than the sensor hardware.
