BMS sensor networks are crossing a threshold: the cost of deploying continuous IAQ sensing across a 30-storey commercial tower has dropped by roughly 40% since 2022. CO₂, PM2.5, temperature, and humidity on a single BACnet MS/TP loop — no proprietary gateway required.

The shift is being driven by three converging trends: (1) code mandates in Singapore (BCA Green Mark 2026), Hong Kong (BEAM Plus 2.0), and Dubai (Al Sa'fat) now require submetered environmental monitoring; (2) MEMS-based CO₂ sensors (NDIR miniaturised) have reached price parity with electrochemical alternatives at scale; (3) specifiers are consolidating on BACnet MS/TP and Modbus RTU for new builds rather than maintaining parallel sensor networks.

What this means for your next specification: design for a minimum of three sensing layers — zone-level CO₂ and temperature, floor-level PM2.5 and VOC, and plant-level airflow and differential pressure — all on a common BMS backbone. Proprietary sensor ecosystems add long-term integration cost with no performance advantage.
