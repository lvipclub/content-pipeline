**The Silent Budget Killer: How Low Delta-T Steals Your Efficiency**

Is your chilled water plant pumping 30–50% more energy than design intent? Low delta-T syndrome — where the supply/return temperature difference narrows — is usually the culprit. It's engineered in through a cascade of small spec choices:

1. **Oversized coils** force two-way valves near-closed, throttling flow until the designed 6.7°C ΔT halves.
2. **Pump curves** get pushed right into low-efficiency zones. Pumping energy rises 30–50%+.
3. **Chillers** run harder: a cold return (10°C vs 12.8°C) degrades kW/ton and risks low-load cycling.

**For your next spec:** right-size coils with realistic entering water temps, specify valves with proper authority (or pressure-independent), pick stable pump curves with differential-pressure reset.
