Your building is probably leaking energy in places no one is watching. Not duct leaks — schedule leaks: VAV boxes running at night, economisers stuck in closed, sensors drifted past their calibration.

Monitoring-based commissioning finds these from BAS data alone. It's the same fault-finding you already do — but systematic, and before the energy bill gets bad:

1. Pull a week of trend data: zone setpoints vs actual, damper commands vs positions, chiller staging vs load
2. Flag anything operating outside its design schedule for more than an hour
3. Fix the top offenders — usually scheduling overrides and stuck dampers — and re-check

ASHRAE 90.1 keeps tightening the baseline, so the days of "it passed commissioning once" are over — measured performance is the new compliance. Buildings with persistent analytics typically cut whole-building energy 5–15% with paybacks under two years [LBNL commissioning study].

The cheapest energy is the energy you never use.
