Specifying VAV boxes? The real decision is the control strategy and damper package — it drives ventilation compliance and energy performance.

Pressure-independent control pairs a flow sensor with a PI loop to hold setpoint despite duct-pressure swings — the right call where deep turndown and ventilation minimums must hold [ASHRAE 62.1]. Pressure-dependent control costs less but lets supply-pressure variation distort airflow at low load.

On dampers, opposed-blade gives better low-flow resolution for modulating VAV service; parallel-blade suits two-position duty. Specify low-leakage class where shutoff matters [ASHRAE 90.1], and allow about 1.5x margin on actuator torque so the blade seats reliably.
