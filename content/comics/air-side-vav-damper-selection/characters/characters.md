# Characters — Bro Woo + Inu Faa (WOOFAA mascot system)

Canonical spec (woofaa-character-system.md, validated 2026-07-30 + 2026-08-04). Embed verbatim in every page prompt.

## Bro Woo
- Young man, adult lean build
- Short black spiky hair — SUBTLE spiky, NOT cartoon lightning-bolt, NOT anime, NOT exaggerated
- Thin rectangular black glasses ALWAYS on
- Grey V-neck sweater over white collared shirt
- Dark grey trousers, black shoes
- Friendly smile, confident engineer-next-door

## Inu Faa
- EXACTLY ONE slim elegant Shiba Inu — NOT chubby, NOT fat
- Tan/cream fur, white chest/muzzle/paws
- Large pointed ears (pink inner), curled fluffy tail
- Yellow collar with small ORANGE lightning-bolt (⚡) charm ALWAYS visible
- Happy attentive expression

## Scene rules (comic adaptation)
- Type A whiteboard lecture: Bro Woo points at whiteboard with pointer; Inu Faa sits beside him (preferred — QA 9/10)
- Type B desk study: avoid for Inu Faa (7/10)
- Type C walking: Bro Woo with tablet + Inu Faa walking alongside (payoff page)
- Forbidden: plant rooms, construction sites, formal meetings for Inu Faa; second dog; missing glasses; business suits; chibi proportions
