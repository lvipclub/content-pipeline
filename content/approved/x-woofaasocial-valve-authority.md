# Topic: Water-side — hydronic balancing, valve authority & the healthy-building energy angle
# Date: Thu 06 Aug 2026

**Audience:** IAQ professionals, WELL consultants, facility managers — healthy buildings / ESG voice

**Draft:**

A building's hydronic system decides both its comfort and its energy column — and most were never balanced. An unbalanced chilled water loop overfeeds the coils near the plant while the far end of the building runs warm, and the pump burns extra kW pushing against it.

Three water-side numbers belong in every healthy-building review:

1. Valve authority ≥ 0.3 — below that, control valves lose their characteristic and comfort swings
2. A documented balancing record — proportional balancing is a commissioning step, not an option
3. Pump kW per L/s from BAS data — pump power follows the affinity laws; halving speed cuts power ~8x [ASHRAE Handbook]

WELL-certifiable comfort is not just air-side. The thermal comfort and energy prerequisites live on the water side too — put balancing in the spec and measure it.
