# Topic: Data centre cooling — supply temperature, aisle containment & ASHRAE TC 9.9 thermal envelopes
# Date: Thu 13 Aug 2026

**Audience:** Specifiers, consultants, facility managers — market intelligence voice

**Hook:**

Data centre cooling is the single largest driver of facility energy use, yet many operators still treat supply temperature as an afterthought. The gap between design intent and operational reality is where PUE goes to die.

Three specification decisions separate a facility that cools within its thermal envelope from one that over-chills its way to a bad PUE:

1. **Aisle containment is the foundational move.** Fully contained cold aisles with blanking panels, brush grommets and sealed doors eliminate recirculation hotspots — which lets you raise supply temperatures without compromising inlet reliability. Containment is a specification item, not a site afterthought [ASHRAE TC 9.9].

2. **The A1-A4 thermal envelopes are the reference framework every specifier should know.** A1 hardware is certified for an 18-27°C supply range; A4 stretches to 45°C for equipment built to tolerate it. As a rule of thumb, each degree of supply temperature rise cuts mechanical cooling load by roughly 2-4%, directly improving PUE. Specify the thermal class of the equipment you buy — the envelope you design for is the envelope you operate in.

3. **Liquid cooling changes the calculus for density.** Direct-to-chip and rear-door heat exchangers break the 10-15 kW per rack air-cooling ceiling, supporting 50-100 kW racks while keeping the air side warm enough that chiller operation is minimal. Plan liquid capacity from day one for high-density zones; retrofitting is expensive and disruptive.

What this means for your next spec: containment first, verified airtight at commissioning; supply setpoints raised incrementally within the envelope your hardware is certified to; liquid cooling capacity planned where density demands it. Disciplined thermal envelope management is the cheapest PUE improvement an existing facility can buy.
